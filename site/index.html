Rep Bell runs the 14-day push-up program for you. It works out every set, spreads them across your day, rings when each one is due, and logs it in one tap on iPhone and Apple Watch.

How it works

Test. Ring. Log.

Your day, your way

Ring or quiet. You pick each morning.

Each program day starts with an alarm at your wake time. Choose Ring and every set rings through silent mode, or Quiet for a day of meetings, with sets as silent banners. Missed sets are counted, never hidden, and the program carries on.

Day 8 and Day 15 are test days, so you see exactly how far you've come.

For days spent sitting

A desk stretch every hour.

Turn on Desk stretch and Rep Bell sends a quiet nudge every hour on weekdays, with a different stretch each time: neck, shoulders, hips, back. It walks you through it, both sides, with a timer.

It's free, it works whether or not a program is running, and it never lands on top of a set.

Workouts

Got five minutes? That's a workout.

Pick Fit my time, choose 5 to 45 minutes, and Rep Bell builds a session for the gear you have and the program day you're on. Or choose from sessions for strength, conditioning, core, balance and stretching, each move shown as you go.

Finished sessions can be saved to Apple Health, with your permission.

Coach voice

A coach that reads your numbers.

After each set, on iPhone and Apple Watch, a short line built from your own counts. Pick the voice you want, or leave it off.
