Support.

Email support@tangomike.app with "Rep Bell" in the subject. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

The bell isn't ringing

In Rep Bell, go to Settings > Alerts, tap Send a test alert, then lock your phone. If it doesn't arrive, allow notifications and alarms for Rep Bell in iPhone Settings, and check that a Focus isn't silencing it. On Ring days, sets ring like an alarm, so also allow Alarms for Rep Bell in iPhone Settings.

I missed a set

That's fine. Missed sets are counted, and the program carries on. Busy for 1 hour turns alerts off for an hour; sets in that hour count as missed.

Something hurts

Stop. Tap Something hurts? on Today to pause the program. Rep Bell is not medical advice; talk to a doctor before starting if you have any health concerns.

Desk stretch

Turn it on in Settings. On weekdays it sends a quiet nudge every hour, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. unless you change the times, and skips an hour when a set is close.

I bought the unlock on another device

Go to Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My data

Rep Bell keeps everything on your devices. See the privacy policy.

What it needs

iPhone with iOS 26 or later; Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later for the Watch app. Coach voice uses Apple Intelligence when it's on, and written lines otherwise.
