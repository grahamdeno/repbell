Privacy.

Short version: Rep Bell collects nothing. There's no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Rep Bell is made by Graham D. This policy covers the Rep Bell app for iPhone and Apple Watch, and this website.

What Rep Bell collects

Nothing. Rep Bell has no account, no server and no analytics, and it contains no advertising or third-party tracking code. Everything you enter and everything the app records stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

What stays on your devices

Your push-up maximum, wake and bed times, the program schedule, the sets you log, your Workouts history and your settings are stored only on your devices. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Rep Bell saves finished Workouts sessions and push-ups to Apple Health. It only writes to Health; it never reads your Health data. You can turn this off at any time in Rep Bell's settings, the Health app or iPhone Settings. Entries already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in Health.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms and alerts for your sets and desk stretches are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Coach voice

If you turn on Coach voice, a short line is written from your own set counts by Apple's on-device model, on your iPhone. Your numbers are not sent to Rep Bell, to Apple or to anyone else.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Rep Bell only receives a confirmation on your device that the unlock is active. Rep Bell never sees your payment details.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. It's hosted by Cloudflare, which sees each request like any website does (your IP address and the kind of device), not anything from the app.

Children

Rep Bell is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
